The product

Franchise partners register online, accept the agreement, and get a signed-off PDF after two approvals.

Applicants fill one form, verify their email and mobile with one-time codes, and accept the active franchise agreement. Accounts reviews first, then a Manager gives final approval. Approval assigns an agreement reference number (FRN/YYYY/NNNN) and generates the agreement PDF automatically. Only a Manager can open it and email it to the franchisee. There are no fees or payments anywhere in the portal.

Pending Verification→ Pending Accounts→ Pending Manager→ Approved + PDF
Rejected by Accounts→ resubmit → Pending Accounts
Rejected by Manager→ resubmit → straight to Pending Manager

Who uses it
Franchise Partner
Registers, verifies, tracks the application, edits and resubmits when changes are asked for, manages their profile and can request deletion of their data.
Accounts
First review: picks up applications, approves or rejects with a reason.
Manager
Final approval; the only role that can open, download and email the agreement PDF.
Admin
Oversight of everything: users, agreement content, settings, duplicates, exports, audit log and data deletion requests. Cannot approve or reject.

Built-in safeguards
  • Every permission is checked on the server, and tests prove each role is refused what it must not see.
  • Two people clicking at once can never both approve, pick up or decide.
  • One registration per PAN and per GST, enforced by the database.
  • Every submission version, acceptance and decision is kept permanently.
  • Append-only audit log of important actions.
  • Locked accounts after repeated wrong passwords; codes are stored only in protected form.
  • DPDP Act consent records and a data deletion process.
  • Exact footer on every PDF page: "This is a computer-generated digital agreement and does not require any physical or digital signature."
